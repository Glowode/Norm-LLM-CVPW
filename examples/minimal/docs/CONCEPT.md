# CONCEPT

The visual half of the brief. Words, not code.

## Palette
Roles the film needs (by name, not hex): background, ink, accent, and any that end a shot differently.

## Master shapes
Two or three shapes that repeat. A film with twelve shapes has none.

## Typography rules
What type is allowed to do, and what it never does. "No outlined or haloed type" is a rule; "make it look nice" is not.

## Texture
What the surface is: paper, glass, grain. One answer, applied everywhere.

## Motion
What moves and how it stops. The most common failure is everything easing at the same rate.
