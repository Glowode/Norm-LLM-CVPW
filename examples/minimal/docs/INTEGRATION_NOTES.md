# INTEGRATION_NOTES

Shared-file requests. `project.json`, `engine/timeline.js`, `engine/main.js`, `engine/lib/`, the manifests,
the styles, `cues/cues.json` and `handoff.json` belong to the integrator (`docs/SPEC.md` S1-S2).

A block author who needs one of those files changed writes the request here and continues on their own range.
The alternative - two agents editing `project.json` in the same afternoon - is how a film acquires a palette
that no longer matches its scenes.
